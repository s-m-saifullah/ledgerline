Money can now be recorded from your phone, not only read.

- Clients: open a client to record a payment from them. One amount is applied to their oldest unpaid services first. On a single service, Record payment settles it, and money over what is owed is saved as a tip. Every payment asks you to confirm before anything is sent.
- Payments can go into an account in another currency. The app shows the rate the two amounts make, uses the stored rate when neither side is US dollars, and lets you set the rate yourself.
- Log service adds a service for a client, with an optional time and due date and the "paid by the hour" helper. A service can be edited or deleted from the same screen.
- Loans: on a person's screen, Record repayment settles part or all of what is open, and the three dots menu lends or borrows more. Each move asks you to confirm first, and the account you used is remembered for next time.
- Writes need a connection. If it drops, what you typed is kept and sending again never records the same move twice.
- If you are still on a temporary password, the app now tells you why nothing loads.

Still on the web for now: writing off or reopening a service, waiving a loan, adding a loan, and changing a payment after it is recorded.

No server update is needed. If you use the in-app update, Settings, Updates shows this version.
