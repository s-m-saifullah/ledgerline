The phone now says things the way the web does, and a budget limit can carry over what is left.

- Set limit has the "Carry over what is left" switch, as on the web. A limit of zero is allowed, and the label and error messages match the web.
- With Hide amounts on, the question before you record a payment, a lend, a borrow or a repayment shows the amount you just typed, so you confirm what you meant. Everything else stays hidden, and a screen reader says "Amount hidden" instead of reading dots.
- One word and one set of messages: "transaction" instead of "entry", and the same sentences when something can't be saved or you are offline.
- Dates and times follow your phone's language and its 24-hour setting. A date shows its year only when it is not this year.
- A client or person screen says it is loading, an empty list shows one plain message, and the Clients and Loans totals look the same.
- Categories ignore a second tap while a change is being saved.

No server update is needed. If you use the in-app update, Settings, Updates shows this version.
