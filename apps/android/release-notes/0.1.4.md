Categories are now on your phone.

- More has a new Categories card. It lists your expense and income categories with their subcategories, with a view filter and a search. Use the menu on a row to edit, archive, unarchive or delete a category.
- Add category creates a main category or a subcategory. Adding, editing, archiving and deleting need a connection and say so when you are offline.
- A category used by entries can't be deleted. The app tells you and offers to archive it instead, which keeps its history.
- Add transaction has a New category button under the Category field. The new category is chosen for your entry straight away.
- Fixed: the search box on Categories no longer jumps down while you type.
