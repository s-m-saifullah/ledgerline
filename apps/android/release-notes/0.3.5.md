Fixes to what the app keeps, how often it syncs and how much work it does. Nothing looks different.

- What you type is kept. A half-typed payment, service, loan or entry survives turning the phone, switching dark mode, changing the font size and even Android closing the app in the background. A payment whose answer was lost is not recorded twice when you try again.
- A change the server rejected can no longer be lost while you fix it, and Sign out no longer gets stuck after an unusual error.
- The Transactions search waits for a pause in your typing, and it ignores accents: "cafe" finds "Café".
- Fewer syncs. A saved change is sent once. While the app is closed it now checks for news once an hour (and only when the battery is not low) instead of every 15 minutes; opening the app still brings everything up to date at once. A change made on another device can therefore take up to an hour to reach the phone while the app is closed.
- Screens stop listening for changes while the app is in the background, and long lists and amounts take less work to draw.
- The update check refuses an answer that is too large, and an update that fails for any reason says so instead of closing the app.

No server update is needed. If you use the in-app update, Settings, Updates shows this version.
