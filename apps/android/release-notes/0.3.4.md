A tidy-up of how the app looks and behaves, with one change to Filters.

- Filters apply as you choose them, as on the web. There is no Apply button: Reset all and Close are at the top of the sheet, and the From and To dates are small pills you tap.
- Needs your attention looks like the other screens, and Discard now asks before it deletes a change you have not sent.
- Questions look the same everywhere: Cancel is outlined, the action is filled, and it is red when it deletes.
- Sheets, menus and text fields share one look. Dialogs and the date picker use the app's own font and sizes, and the three-dot buttons on Categories line up at the right.
- Updated libraries.

No server update is needed. If you use the in-app update, Settings, Updates shows this version.
