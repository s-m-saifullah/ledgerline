# ADR 0047, early amendments

Amendments to [ADR 0047](0047-web-redesign.md) from the first pull requests of the web redesign, moved unchanged so the ADR stays short. Newer amendments are in the ADR itself.

- **Foundation (2026-10-29).** The shared pieces are in `packages/ui` (`pieces.tsx`, `pieces.css`; no router and no money: a link is a `render` prop, an amount a node) and the overlay, top bar and month control in `apps/web/src/components`. New classes start with `ui-`; the old radius tokens stay until the last old screen moves; the new shapes are `--shape-field/card/hero/sheet`. One layout system, `layout.css`: the body is a container named `app`, and 700 and 1100 px are the only widths new CSS may use (a test checks it). `useIsPhone` goes with the last screen that uses it (Categories, PR 12). The phone warns at 90 per cent of a limit (the prototype said 85).

- **Shell (2026-10-29).** One `aside` is the rail and the sidebar (hidden under 700 px, 88 px from 700, 248 px from 1100); the phone has a top bar, five tabs and one Add button, which adds a transaction except on More, Settings and Import; a page with its own add (Clients, then Accounts and the rest) registers it through a small hook the shell reads. The top bar shows Synced, Updating or Offline from the live-updates poller (on a phone only when not Synced).

- **Transactions pull request (2026-10-30).** After the owner's browser check, the Filters sheet changed from the phone's rule: **each change applies at once** (there is no Apply button; the sheet closes with Done, Reset all applies too, and the history keeps one entry for the sheet, not one per click). A date applies once it is a whole date, and an end before the start applies nothing and says so. **Account, Category and Tag are combo boxes**: type to narrow the list, "All …" clears it, and a category or a tag that does not exist offers to be created (a category as expense or income, a tag as it is) and is applied once saved; an account is never created from here, and a viewer is offered nothing to create. The search says what it searches ("Search payee, payer or note"). **Clear filters** moved from the chip row to the head, beside Export CSV, because at 390 px it wrapped the chips to a second line when it appeared. While a new search loads the rows stay (dimmed) instead of a loading line, so the page does not jump. Kind chips, search and the sheet are applied to the same address filters as before. The phone still keeps its Apply, and its sheet is not changed here.

- **Entry editor pull request (2026-10-30).** Add and Edit are one sheet (a sheet on a phone, a drawer from 700 px) with Cancel or Close in the head and Save pinned at the foot, sharing one set of fields. The type is a **pill**: Expense, Income and, on a new entry, Transfer (it replaces the "Add mode" and "Entry type" radios; Edit shows Expense and Income only, fixed for a transfer's fee or bonus and a payment's tip). Choosing Transfer shows the transfer form in the same sheet. The amount is large with its currency code beside it. One card holds payee (payer for income), account, category (with the split) and when (date and time, side by side from 700 px, stacked on a phone so neither is cut); a second holds tags, note and **Pending as a switch** (off saves as cleared). "More details" is gone: time, payee, note and tags are always shown. **Delete asks in a centred question** ("Delete this entry?", Keep entry, Confirm delete). Account stays a native select; the help sentences under the amount and the status are gone. **The category is the Filters sheet's combo box** (also on every split line): type to narrow, and a name nobody has yet offers "Create expense category “name”" (income for income), which replaces "New category" and works in Edit too; an answer that never came keeps the same request, so choosing the suggestion again replays it instead of making a second category. **Opening a sheet never shows a placeholder dialog**: a quick load shows nothing, a slow one a plain sheet, a failure shows at once with Try again.

- **Transfer editor pull request (2026-10-31).** The transfer is laid out like the entry sheet, in the same sheet or drawer: the big amount with its currency code (between two currencies a second one for what arrives, with the rate line and the hand-rate box); one card with **From account, a Swap button, To account**, date and time; on a new transfer a card with the fee, the bonus (a **switch row**, the account's rate as its hint) and, under **More** (a disclosure that opens itself when a category is missing), their categories; one card with tags and note. **Swap** (new on the web) exchanges the accounts and, between two currencies, the two amounts, clears a rate set by hand, re-runs the fee and bonus prefill, and is off with an archived account, with fewer than two accounts and for a viewer. **Delete asks in a centred question** ("Delete this transfer?") holding "Also delete its bonus entry" and "Also delete its fee entry" (each only when it exists, ticked by default); Undo is unchanged. A saved transfer has no type pill or fee and bonus fields. Unchanged: replay with the same key after an unconfirmed result, Close for now, Reload latest details, Retry, read-only for viewers, archived accounts kept on an edit. The old transfer-form classes are deleted; `transfer-fee` stays for the recurring rule editor.

- **Import and export (2026-10-31, PR 6a).** One page, from the approved prototype (the route stays `/import`; the palette still offers "Import from CSV"): the steps are a line above both columns, the import on the left and the export on the right (2 to 1 from 1100 px, equal from 700 px, stacked on a phone). A new shared **pick row** (`components/pick-row.tsx`: label left, choice and chevron right, a native select over the row) serves "Import into", every column, the date format, the number style and every category choice. **File:** the account row, a dashed drop zone, the template, Recent imports (a row opens the Undo question). **Columns:** the first rows as headings, switch rows, the amount style as a segmented control, "Also in the file" closed. **Categories:** a pick row per unmatched name. **Review:** three count cards, New tags, Possible duplicates, Left out, "Show the N ready rows". **Done:** Imported N entries, View in Transactions, Import another file, Undo. **Export** is a card on every step (From, To, three downloads). **Undo asks in a centred question**, with the server's message inside it when it fails. Unchanged: every error message, the 5 MB and 5,000-row limits, the file never leaving the browser, the duplicate rules, the template. The old `import-*`, `export-range` and `export-actions` CSS is deleted; `export-inline` stays for Transactions.
- **More and Settings pull request (2026-10-31, PR 6b).** Built from the approved prototype (`R.more`, `R.settings`, its three overlays). **More:** three groups of rows (one column on a phone; Money setup the right column from a 700 px page) with a hint only where there is something to say: Clients and Loans show who owes at the end of the row from Home's summary (left out at zero; Loans says "People owe you", as the summary has no count of people), Settings carries the version. **Settings:** two columns from a 700 px page; the theme is a segmented control (group still named Theme); the two device settings are switch rows; the time zone is a row with a bordered select that **saves as soon as a zone is chosen** (off for a viewer); one Import and export row; a red Sign out button (the phone and tablet top-bar icon is gone). **Two-factor** is the prototype's rows with no Manage disclosure; each opens **one sheet that changes step in place** (password, the scan for a new setup, the backup codes with Done pinned); the codes step cannot be dismissed (the overlay gained `dismissible`). **Devices** are rows with a short "Last used" date and a 44 px Sign out; **Sign out all other devices asks in a centred question**. Notices use the existing toast. On a narrow phone titles and hints wrap instead of being cut. Unchanged: every message, the password and code checks, idempotent saves, the ten-device limit.
- **Budgets (2026-10-31, PR 7).** From the approved prototype (`R.budgets`, `OV.setlimit`). The month control is in the page head from 700 px and under the top bar on a phone. The **Left this month** hero (red when over; a light bar; "N% used", with the days left in the current month only) sits beside one list from 1100 px (5 to 7). A row is avatar, name, what is left or over, a bar and one hint ("$A of $B", B counting what was carried in, then "Carried over" or "Overspent"), overspent first, then most spent. **Every category without a budget folds into one line** (the prototype's rule); a writer taps its rows to set a limit, and an empty month shows Copy from last month with the fold open. **Set limit** is the shared sheet: the category and what it spent, the entry sheet's big amount field, four round amounts, the carry-over switch, **Remove limit in one tap with a toast, no question (as the prototype)**, and Save ("Limit saved."). A refused or conflicting save starts a new request on the next try. New shared: the hero's red tone, meter and note; the big amount moved to `components/entry-amount`.
- **Insights (2026-10-31, PR 8).** From the approved prototype (`R.insights`). The month control is the shared `components/month-head` (Budgets uses it too). **This month** is a bar of spent against kept (red when more went out than came in; none without money in) and three figures that wrap whole. **Spending** is a bar split by category, then a row each (the first five in the chart colours, the rest neutral); a main category opens to its subcategories and "Directly in {Parent}", and every row links to Transactions. **Money in and out** is drawn only with two or more active months. **Net worth now** (assets and liabilities from the account balances, archived included; left out with more than one currency) replaces the Accounts page's Net worth card. **Both charts and their figures begin on the month the ledger started to be used** (the net worth report's `historyStart`), up to twelve months, at the right. One **Show the figures** at the foot holds both tables. Bars and keys are pills (the shape contract allows nothing smaller).
