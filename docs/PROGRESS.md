- **2026-11-21:** Android: **what is typed on the phone survives Android ending the app** (merged as #342; Android only; not released: it ships with the next `android-v*` tag, on the owner's word; no server change; [ADR 0056](adr/0056-typed-text-survives-the-app-being-ended.md)). WP-18b, the second half of WP-18. **Before:** Android may end the app in the background, and the phone's sheets lived only in view models, so a half-typed payment, service, loan or entry was gone, and so was the retry key of a payment whose answer had been lost (sent again, it would get a new key and could be recorded twice). **Now:** Record payment (service and client), Log or edit service, Lend, Borrow and Repay, and the transaction editor (including correcting a rejected change) keep their form, and what the sheet can choose from, in the activity's saved state on every change (`Draft` and `keepDraft` in `drafts/`, written the moment the form changes) and take it back when the view model is created again; Clients and Loans keep which client or person is open, so a sheet has a screen; `WriteKeys` keeps its keys beside the form under a hash of the change (never its text), so the retry after a restart carries the key of the first try. A draft records its ledger and is dropped, with its keys, if the phone's ledger is another (a shared phone never shows the last person's text); closing a sheet, a payment going through, a saved entry and leaving the screen forget it, a rotation does not. The last rate look-up is not kept: a restored cross-currency sheet asks again. **Proof:** tests first (a restored saved-state handle stands for the restart: typed form back, same key on retry, nothing back after close or for another ledger, for each sheet, the editor, the open client and person); `DraftsAreKeptTest` reads the sources so a new money sheet must keep its draft and keys (the delete question and the Budgets limit sheet are its listed exceptions); 40 deliberate breaks, the first pass left three (the rate look-up after a restore on two sheets, a draft laid over a read-only entry), closed with tests, all caught on rerun; the debug and release builds (Hilt takes the new parameters, R8 keeps the draft types), the full Android unit suite, lint and the R8 check, run as CI runs them. **Not covered:** the Budgets limit sheet, the category sheets, Filters and the delete questions keep nothing across a restart. **Not checked on a phone** (the owner skipped it for now): type into a payment and a loan, kill the app from the recents screen, reopen it. **Next:** WP-19, phone speed and search.
