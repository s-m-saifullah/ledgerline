- **2026-10-31:** Web redesign, PR 5c of [ADR 0047](adr/0047-web-redesign.md): the **transfer editor** (merged as #204 after the owner's browser check). A new or saved transfer is laid out like the entry sheet, in the same sheet or drawer, with the pill in the head when adding. The amount is large with its currency code, and between two currencies a second large amount says what arrives, with the rate line and the hand-rate box under it. One card holds **From account, a Swap button, To account**, date and time (side by side from 700 px). **Swap** is new on the web: it exchanges the accounts and, between two currencies, the two amounts (each figure stays with its own currency), clears a rate set by hand, lets the fee and bonus start again from the accounts that are now From and To, shows form messages again only when a save was already tried, and is off with an archived account, with fewer than two accounts and for a viewer. A new transfer has one more card: the fee (one hint line and the preview of what leaves the account), the bonus as a **switch** ("Add a bonus", the account's rate as its hint, then the bonus amount) and **More** (fee and bonus categories), which stays a disclosure and opens itself when a category is needed. A last card holds tags and note, and on a saved transfer one short line each about a fee or bonus entry that is edited from the list. **Delete asks in a centred question** ("Delete this transfer?", Keep transfer, Confirm delete) holding the "Also delete its bonus entry" and "Also delete its fee entry" boxes, each only when that entry exists; the footer's two-step is gone. Unchanged on purpose: save and delete replay with the same key after an unconfirmed result, Close for now, Reload latest details, the retry buttons, read-only for viewers, archived accounts kept on an edit, Undo. Changed on purpose: the fee's help sentence (`transfer-fee` spec and unit test), the notes about a fee or bonus entry (unit test), the bonus is a switch (the specs now toggle it as a switch), Confirm delete lives in the question dialog (`transfers` spec), and the `disclosures` spec waits for the sheet to settle before it measures (it read 43.99 px mid slide-in on a phone, an intermittent failure that was there before). New: `transfer-sheet.test.tsx` (swap in one and two currencies, the hand rate, the form's messages, archived and read-only, the question and its boxes) and `tests/e2e/transfer-sheet.spec.ts` (widths 320, 390 and 1280, Swap between From and To, pinned Save, 44 px targets, the question, axe in both themes).
