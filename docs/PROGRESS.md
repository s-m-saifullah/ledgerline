- **2026-11-10:** Android, slice 4b-1 of [ADR 0050](adr/0050-money-writes-on-the-phone.md): a payment into an account in another currency, where one side is USD, for a service and for a client (Android only; no server change; checked on the owner's phone over wireless debugging against a scratch server; merged as #251 after the owner's phone check; not released). **What it does:** a dollar service can now go into any open account (a euro account, say) and a euro service into a dollar account; before, only accounts in the service's own currency were offered. Such an account adds **Amount received** (in the account's currency, under the Account and Category rows), a line with the rate the two amounts fix (\"1 USD = 0.911111 EUR\", so a typo in the amount received stands out), and a **Tip** field in the fold, in the account's currency. Money over what is owed is no longer turned into a tip on these payments (the web's rule; the server refuses an amount over what is owed). The question says what arrived and what it settles (\"Record €410.00 from Northwind Design into Euro account. It settles $450.00.\"). The write is the same one: `receivedAmount` (and the tip) in the account's currency; no rate is sent, because the server works out the rate it saves from the two amounts and checks them again. For a client the amount received is shared across the services the amount pays. **Left out on purpose (slice 4b-2):** accounts where neither side is USD (the stored rate looked up, or one set by hand): those are not offered, and the sheet says \"Some accounts need a rate set by hand: use the web for now.\" That hint's words changed, and the old \"Accounts in other currencies\" tests changed on purpose. **Rate math:** `deriveRate`, `invertRate` and the sentence are ported to Kotlin (exact integer arithmetic) and checked against 155 cases generated from the web's own `packages/shared` functions (`rate-parity.json`); **lint found a real bug the unit tests could not**: `BigInteger.TWO` needs Android 13 and the app supports Android 11, so the rate line would have crashed there (now `BigInteger.valueOf(2)`). **Shared pieces:** `CrossCurrency` (pure: the amount received, the tip, the rate, which accounts fit) and the shared fields in both sheets. **Departures from the prototype:** the tip field sits in the fold as in `OV.receive`, titled \"Tip, date, time and note\" only when an account in another currency needs it; the rate line and the amount received sit under the account rows. **Tests:** the Android suite is 1,812 tests and passes, with lint. 49 deliberate mutations: the first 46 caught 43; the survivors were two gaps in my tests (the old tip line must stay hidden when a tip is typed for another currency) and one in the fixture (an inverted rate of 100,000 or more with a fraction, which only the decimals floor shows), all now caught. Screenshots at 320 and 390 dp, light and dark, and with the largest text, were reviewed. **Phone check:** a dollar service paid into a euro account (the rate line, the tip in euros, the question, a missing amount refused), a euro service paid into a dollar account (the pound account left out, with the hint), and a client who owes in dollars and euros (the Currency row) all behaved as expected; the server then held $300.00 settled by €275.00 with a €5.00 tip at a rate of 1.0909 that it worked out itself, and €150.00 settled by $160.00, with no duplicate. - **2026-11-10:** A flaky Android sign-in test fixed (test only; no app change; in review as #250). `SignInViewModelTest.theAccountIsAskedAboutWithTheNewTokenBeforeTheSessionHasIt` (slice 2 of [ADR 0050](adr/0050-money-writes-on-the-phone.md)) failed once on a pull request's check and passed on a rerun of the same commit. **Cause:** the session enters its offer state on the network thread while the view model is still running there, so the test's watcher of session states is resumed after the screen has finished, and the test thread could compare its list first (a slow runner makes that likely). **Reproduced** by making the watcher slow (300 ms): the old test failed every time. **Fix:** the watcher keeps its states in a list that is safe to read from another thread, and the test waits for the states it expects before comparing; the check that the session never offered a token now waits for the first state and pauses 300 ms before saying none came. With the slow watcher the test passes, and it passed three normal runs; nothing in the app changed.
