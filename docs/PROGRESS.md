- **2026-11-13:** Web: **width queries in `styles.css` are container queries** (merged as #292; web only; not released: it ships with the next server tag; no migration, API contract unchanged). Fifth part of the styles clean-up, checked by the owner in the browser. The nine viewport `max-width: 700px` queries became `@container app (max-width: 699.98px)`, the frame's 1000 px step moved to 1100 px (a window 1001 to 1099 px wide has 32 px of page padding instead of 48), and the date picker's 400 px rule became a 6 px `clamp()` padding; `styles.css` joined the contract for the widths and any viewport `@media` width is refused in every stylesheet. **A bug found on the way:** the date picker had always cut "November" to "Novemb" at 401 px and wider (the old 4 px padding rule only hid it below 401 px); with 6 px padding the month select has room for "September", and a spec measures the longest month name at 360, 390, 412, 700 and 1280 px (it failed at all five first); at 320 px the picker still cannot fit it, as before. **What moved:** at exactly 700 px the tablet rules now apply (phone rules used to linger there, out of step with the rail); everything else is identical: 35 of 48 computed frame, toast and field measurements at six widths were unchanged and the 13 that differ are exactly those. **Proof:** the contract rule red first, 8 mutations all caught, computed values at six widths and screenshots before and after, the full browser suite and the pull request's own check. The mobile `splits` spec failed once in the local full run: it fails about 6 in 10 repeated runs even at the commit from before this work (an existing flake: a closing sheet and the next one coexist briefly). **Next in the clean-up:** the page frame itself (`<main>` onto `ui-page`, the visible part), then the date picker on the shared overlay.
