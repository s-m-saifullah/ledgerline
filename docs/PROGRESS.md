Read this at the start of every session and update it when a piece of work lands. It records the state of the project and what comes next. Scope and design live in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md); decisions in [adr/](adr/). Keep it free of personal details, real hostnames and credentials.

## Session log

- **2026-10-17:** Lending and borrowing, step 1 of 3, the API (ADR 0030, API 0.1.32, migration 0022): each client can have one running balance per currency (an account of the `loan` type linked to the client; positive means they owe you, negative means you owe them). `POST /contacts/{id}/loans/{lend|borrow|repay|forgive|opening}` saves ordinary transfers between one of your accounts and that balance, a repayment that follows the balance's sign and cannot exceed what is open, a forgiveness entry (expense or income, in a category you choose) for what is left, or an existing loan as the balance before Ledgerline without moving money anywhere. `GET .../loans` lists the balances. Those accounts cannot be edited, archived or deleted by hand and a client that has one cannot be deleted. The backup manifest and restore drill gain a `loans` check and a `loan_links` invariant (scratch drill and 14 negative cases passed; the compose project was removed).
