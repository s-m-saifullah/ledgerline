WP-19a of the review's fixes, the first of three parts of WP-19 (phone speed and search). **Before:** every letter typed in the search box started a read of the whole filtered ledger (the search is matched in Kotlin) and a synchronous `SharedPreferences.commit` on the main thread, and "cafe" did not find "Café". **Now:** the box shows each letter at once and the list is read after 250 ms without typing (`SearchPauseMillis`); a chip, a date, the account, clearing the box and filters restored on opening apply at once; the kept filters are written in the background while typing, and clearing them (a sign-out) still finishes before it returns. The match ignores the accents of Latin letters on both sides ("cafe" finds "Café", "café" finds "cafe"), through one function (`withoutAccents`) that serves the mirror's rows and the entries an unsent change rebuilds; a Cyrillic "й" stays apart from "и" and a Korean syllable is not matched by part of its letters; letters with no accent form ("ł", "đ") stay as they are. **No migration, on purpose:** the audit's stored search column would miss an unsent edit of a payee or note (it exists only in the outbox), and one search over 20,000 entries took about 125 ms on a development machine (a plain list read about 4 ms; a phone will be several times slower, but it now runs once per pause); if it ever feels slow on the phone, that number is where to revisit the column. **Tests changed on purpose:** `anAccentIsPartOfTheLetterOnlyTheCaseIsIgnored` recorded the earlier decision not to fold accents; two other search expectations and one view-model test (it advances the clock past the pause) moved with it. **Proof:** each new test failed first; 13 deliberate breaks, all caught after two redundant rules the first pass exposed were removed; the full Android unit suite, lint, the debug and release builds and the R8 check. The "Known gaps" clause about accents is gone. **Not checked on a phone** (the owner skipped it for now). **Next:** WP-19b, the sync and network costs.
